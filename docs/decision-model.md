# Spring-to-Farm Decision Model

JALCHAKRA AI connects spring revival planning with downstream agricultural decisions.

## Decision Chain

~~~text
Spring Health
    ↓
Recharge Opportunity
    ↓
Expected Water Recovery
    ↓
Seasonal Water Availability
    ↓
Water Stress / Debt
    ↓
Intervention Scenario
    ↓
Farm Water Allocation
    ↓
Impact Monitoring
~~~

## Why the Chain Matters

A recharge intervention should not be evaluated only by its construction output. The planning workflow also needs to consider expected water availability, seasonal demand, feasible farm use, and measurable outcomes.

## Prototype Boundaries

The current hackathon prototype uses planning logic and simulated/demo inputs where live field data is unavailable. Production deployment would require field validation, calibrated hydrological models, reliable geospatial datasets, and continuous observation feedback.

## Key Output

The platform should help a user answer three practical questions:

1. Where should recharge action be considered?
2. What water change could the scenario produce?
3. How can the resulting water availability inform farm planning?
