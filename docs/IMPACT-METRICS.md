# JALCHAKRA AI — Impact Metrics

## Why this layer matters

JALCHAKRA AI should measure more than revived spring discharge. The platform connects spring health to farm water availability, food security, and tribal livelihood resilience.

## Core indicators

| Indicator | What it measures |
|---|---|
| Spring Recovery Index | Relative improvement in spring discharge and reliability |
| Recharge Efficiency | Expected water gain per recharge intervention |
| Farm Water Security | Share of planned crop demand that can be safely met |
| Crop Resilience Score | Stability of crop planning under water uncertainty |
| Livelihood Resilience | Estimated reduction in income risk from water stress |
| Intervention ROI | Expected impact compared with intervention cost |

## Decision rule

The platform should prefer interventions that improve long-term water reliability while protecting downstream agricultural demand. A technically successful recharge action is not considered optimal if it creates a new water deficit for farms or households.

## Monitoring cycle

`Observe → Predict → Simulate → Recommend → Intervene → Measure → Learn`

This closes the AI feedback loop and lets future recommendations learn from observed outcomes.
