# Spring-to-Farm Decision Model

JALCHAKRA AI connects spring revival planning with downstream farm decisions.

## Decision Chain

Spring health
→ recharge opportunity
→ available-water estimate
→ intervention scenario
→ future water availability
→ farm water requirement
→ feasible crop/allocation decision
→ monitoring feedback

## Why the Chain Matters

A spring-revival plan should not stop at identifying a recharge intervention. The expected change in water availability can also affect agricultural planning. The platform therefore keeps spring, water, intervention, and farm-planning signals connected.

## Prototype Boundary

The current repository demonstrates a software prototype with planning foundations and simulated/demo data. Live field sensors, production geospatial datasets, and field-calibrated models remain future extensions.

## Next Engineering Steps

- Improve validation of planning assumptions.
- Connect observations to scenario inputs.
- Calibrate forecasts with field data when available.
- Make farm recommendations traceable to the water-availability assumptions used.
