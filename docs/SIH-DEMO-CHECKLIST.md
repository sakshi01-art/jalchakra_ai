# SIH Demo Checklist

Use this checklist to keep the JALCHAKRA AI demonstration focused on one continuous decision story.

- [ ] Select a representative spring
- [ ] Show Spring DNA health signals
- [ ] Open the Recharge Map and identify a priority area
- [ ] Show the Water Debt demand/supply gap
- [ ] Run a Future Simulator scenario
- [ ] Review ranked intervention options
- [ ] Translate expected water availability in the Reverse Farm Planner
- [ ] Show the Impact Chain
- [ ] Demonstrate Monitoring and feedback
- [ ] Generate the final planning report

## Presentation Tip

Keep the narrative centered on the chain:

**Spring decline → water stress → recharge decision → future water availability → farm resilience.**
