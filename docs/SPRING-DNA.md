# Spring DNA Intelligence

JALCHAKRA AI models each spring as a dynamic system rather than a single water point.

## Inputs
- seasonal discharge observations
- rainfall and recharge indicators
- terrain and land-use signals
- nearby agriculture and demand patterns

## Output
The Spring DNA layer produces a compact health profile that can be compared across seasons and used by downstream recharge and planning modules.

## Decision principle
> Diagnose the spring system first, then recommend an intervention.
