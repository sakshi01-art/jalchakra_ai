# AI & Decision Intelligence

JALCHAKRA AI is designed as a planning system where AI supports diagnosis, prioritization and scenario comparison.

## Intelligence Layers

1. **Spring Health Scoring** — combines observed spring indicators into a structured health profile.
2. **Recharge Prioritization** — ranks candidate intervention zones using available spatial and environmental signals.
3. **Scenario Simulation** — compares possible outcomes under different rainfall, budget and intervention assumptions.
4. **Intervention Ranking** — evaluates feasible intervention packages against planning constraints.
5. **Farm Translation** — converts expected water availability into practical farm-planning signals.

## Guardrails

The prototype should distinguish observed, estimated and simulated values. AI recommendations are decision-support outputs and should be validated with field observations before implementation.
